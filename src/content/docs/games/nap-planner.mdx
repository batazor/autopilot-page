Plan your server's **NAP (non-aggression pact)** for the Sunfire Castle season
without spreadsheets: the map below shows the real territory layout — the
castle, 4 turrets, 4 citadels (strongholds), 12 fortresses and all 74 buff
towers at their actual kingdom-map coordinates (identical on every server).

- **Add alliances** on the right (tag + color), select one, then **click
  objects** on the map or in the list to assign them. Click again to unassign.
- **Buff towers** are an optional layer — tower dots are colored by buff type
  (see the legend); an assigned tower keeps its buff color as a ring.
- **Risk marks**: pick the 🎯 snipe / 👁️ watch / ⚠️ risk brush and click an
  object to flag it; **double-click** any object to attach a note (shown in
  its tooltip).
- Everything stays **in your browser** (autosaved locally). Share the plan
  with other leads via **Copy link** (the whole plan travels inside the URL —
  no files, no server), **PNG** (map snapshot with the alliance legend, ready
  for Discord/Telegram), or **Download JSON** / **Import JSON** for backups.
