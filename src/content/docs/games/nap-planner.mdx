Plan your server's **NAP (non-aggression pact)** without spreadsheets: divide
the 4 citadels, 12 fortresses and all 74 buff facilities between alliances at
their real kingdom-map coordinates (identical on every server; the castle in
the centre is just a landmark — it is not part of the division).

- **Map or Table** — paint objects on the 45° in-game-style map, or switch to
  the roster table (object / owner / buff / mark / note / handovers) and set
  everything with dropdowns, spreadsheet-style.
- **Add alliances** (tag + color), select one, then click objects to assign;
  click again to unassign. Click with no tool selected (or double-click) to
  open the **object card** — owner, marks, note and this object's handover
  schedule in one place.
- **Schedule**: plan handovers (who takes which fort, from what date); pick a
  date to preview the map as of that day — scheduled objects carry a ⟳ badge.
- **Who gets what**: the stats block shows per-alliance counts and summed
  facility buffs, plus an Unassigned row with what is still up for grabs; the
  legend counts division progress per buff type.
- Everything stays **in your browser** (autosaved locally). Share via **Copy
  link** (the whole plan travels inside the URL), **PNG**, or **Download
  JSON** / **Import JSON**. UI speaks **RU/EN**.
